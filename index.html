Encouragement inspired by two Ikeda Sensei readings

There is still time to create a new beginning.

Dear friends, we have only about a month and a half left in 2026. Some of us may have achieved our goals, while others may be facing difficulties or wondering if there is enough time.

The two articles featuring Ikeda Sensei’s guidance on this page remind us that the present moment is precious. We do not have to wait until the new year to make a fresh determination. We can chant Nam-myoho-renge-kyo with a clear purpose and take concrete action today.

Although not every goal will be completed by December 31, each courageous step can become a meaningful cause for future victory. We can also create hope through sincere encouragement of others.

Don't count the days left in 2026. Make every day count toward your victory.Original encouragement for this personal study site — not a quotation from Ikeda Sensei

Let us make these remaining weeks a time of daimoku, determination, compassionate action, and progress in our human revolution.

A personal year-end challenge

Four actions to begin today

  1. Choose one meaningful goal. Focus on an area of life you want to challenge.
  2. Make a fresh determination. Chant Nam-myoho-renge-kyo (daimoku) with conviction.
  3. Take concrete action. Turn your determination into a step today, however small.
  4. Encourage someone. Listen, share hope, and advance together in the spirit of kosen-rufu.
Selections for reading together

Read aloud with members

Follow along with the highlighted passages from World Tribune and Living Buddhism. The selections below also appear in their respective article pages.

1 & 3 · Treasure the present moment and start anew

World Tribune · November 13, 2026 · “Time Without Beginning” · p. 3 · View in original article

Read aloud · Passage 1For us, every day in our lives is time without beginning. We are able to make our beings brim with the boundless life force of time without beginning. Every day, we make a fresh departure from the starting point of life—time without beginning.

Read aloud · Passage 2That’s why the present moment is the most important. We mustn’t dwell on the past. There is no need to. To put all our energy into the present moment, with great hope for the future—that is the mark of a person who is wise in the way of living.

Read aloud · Passage 3This moment is time without beginning. Everything starts from now. The past no longer exists. The future isn’t here yet. All that exists is this present moment. And in a flash, the present becomes the past. It both exists and doesn’t exist. It is empty, or ku, the state of nonsubstantiality. In this state, life continues, moment to moment. It exists nowhere aside from the moment. We experience happiness and unhappiness only in the moment.

2 · Create the right time through action

Living Buddhism · November 2026 · “The Time for Worldwide Kosen-rufu, for World Peace” · p. 37 · View in original article

Read aloud · Create the right timeWithout positive action, there is a danger of losing all direction. A strong determination to move the times toward solid good is the driving force that propels us to create the right time. The “time” is not simply an objective condition; it is carved, in essence, by a firm will. In other words, a sound age is created by the will to fight and win, day after day, steadily and surely, no matter whether anyone is watching our efforts or not. Mr. Toda said: “We should focus our efforts on fostering one new sincere member, and then foster another, and another. This is what creates the time.” In this sense, the right time is not something that we wait for, but something that we ourselves must create. This is what I learned from my mentor during my youthful struggles at his side.
